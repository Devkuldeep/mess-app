# Competitors (mess/tiffin software)

1. **Indian Mesh Tiffin Management System** sabse purana direct competitor hai. Yeh software office jaane waalon ko daily tiffin dene wale sellers ke liye bana hai, aur isme customer info sambhalna aur alag-alag messaging platforms par invoice bhejna jaisi cheezein hain. Company 2010 me Zirakpur (Punjab) me bani thi. Iske features me delivery boy management aur banquet management bhi aate hain.

2. **MealPe** hostel, PG, staff aur canteen mess par focus karta hai. Iske features me RSVP meals, attendance tracking, plate count aur menu scheduling hain, aur QR scan karke advance meal book hota hai. Yeh hostel, PG aur coliving mess operators, hospital canteen aur cloud kitchens ko target karta hai.

3. **SpaceBasic** bade colleges aur universities ke hostels ke liye hai. Isme digital cafeteria, menu aur meal bookings, AI analytics aur campus payments ka system hai. Yeh enterprise level ka hai, to chhote mess walon ke liye mehnga ya bhaari ho sakta hai.

4. **TiffinHub** chhote tiffin owners ke liye kaafi nazdeeki product lagta hai. Iska dawa hai ki yeh customers track karta hai, orders manage karta hai, WhatsApp payment reminders chalata hai aur inventory sambhalta hai, free trial ke saath. Yeh tumhare MVP se sabse zyada match karta hai.

5. **Mess Manager (CommandHQ)** ek Play Store app hai jo mess management ke liye hai (Coimbatore ki company). Isme poora feature detail mujhe nahi mila, par yeh chhote level par seedha competitor hai.

## Inke alawa jo bhi dhyan me rakhne laayak hain

- **PG software:** TrackMyPG ₹299/month se shuru hota hai aur tenants, rent, invoice aur expenses ek dashboard me sambhalta hai. Mess ka kaam wahan side feature hai, par PG owners ke liye woh option ban sakta hai.
- **Foreign aur ready-made:** Tiffinware ($99 per month flat se shuru), Deonde aur Rekart jaise tiffin software, jo zyada tar naye aur international market ko target karte hain.
- **Customer-side apps:** TiffinMitra jaise apps. Yeh customers ko multiple tiffin providers ke subscription, leave aur meal usage ek jagah dikhate hain, aur nearby providers dhundhne dete hain. Yeh mess owners ko software nahi bechte, par customer ka dhyan kheench sakte hain.
- **Bade players:** Swiggy ne tiffin subscription me entry li thi. Swiggy Daily ₹50 per meal se shuru hua tha aur shuru me Gurgaon me live tha. Yeh khabar purani hai, to abhi ka status alag ho sakta hai.
- **Sabse bada "competitor":** WhatsApp, copy aur Excel. Bahut se tiffin providers abhi bhi spreadsheet, WhatsApp aur notebook par kaam karte hain. Asli ladai inhi purani aadaton se hai.

## Tumhare liye mauka kahan hai

Jo results mile unme main in cheezon ka saaf combination nahi dekh paaya (kam se kam unke public pages par):

- **Reseller-based white-label model** jisme students resell karein aur commission kamayein.
- **Wastage tracking + festival-wise demand forecast** jo chhote mess ke liye bana ho.
- **Hindi aur voice/WhatsApp first** entry jo kam padhe-likhe owners ke liye ho.
- **Owner ke liye mini courses** (Growth Academy).

Yeh meri limited search par based hai, aur companies ke andar ke features alag ho sakte hain. Isliye tumhare liye sabse accha kadam yeh hoga ki TiffinHub, Indian Mesh aur MealPe ke free trial ya demo khud lo, aur dekho ki unme kya kamzor hai.
