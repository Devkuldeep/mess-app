# In-app "Mess Growth Academy" (mini courses)

Yeh feature owners ko app se jodkar rakhega aur tumhare resellers ka support ka bojh bhi kam karega.

## Format: kaise dena hai

- **Chhote lessons:** har lesson 3-5 minute ka video (Hindi aur local language me) ya ek page ka text, kyunki owners ke paas lamba time nahi hota.
- **Ek course me 5-7 lessons,** aur har lesson ke end me ek chhota **action task** (jaise "aaj apne 5 customers se feedback maango").
- **Progress aur badges:** course complete karne par badge ya certificate ("Certified Mess Owner"), jo woh apne mess ke page par dikha sakein.
- **App ke saath jodo:** jaise wastage wala lesson dekhte hi uske neeche "Apna wastage tracker kholo" ka button.
- **Offline aur WhatsApp:** lesson ka summary WhatsApp par bhi bhejo, taaki woh baad me dekh sakein.

## Top 6 course ideas

1. **Mess shuru karna aur pehle 30 customer laana:** pricing kaise rakhein, trial tiffin offer, WhatsApp group aur local marketing.
2. **Wastage aur kharcha kam karna:** quantity ka sahi andaaza, mandi se khareedne ke tips, recipe costing aur profit nikaalna.
3. **Customer retention:** skip kam karwana, feedback se sudhaar, loyalty rewards aur churn rokna.
4. **Hygiene aur FSSAI:** licence kaise lein, kitchen checklist aur customer ka bharosa kaise banaye.
5. **Marketing basics:** Google Maps listing, Instagram aur WhatsApp status, referral offer, festival campaigns.
6. **Paisa sambhalna:** udhaar aur recovery, monthly hisaab, GST basics aur kab expand karna chahiye.

Bonus course: **"Festival season ke liye taiyaari"** jo seedha app ke festival calendar se jude.

## Isse tumhe kya fayda hoga

- **Retention:** jo owner course se seekhta hai aur uska business badhta hai, woh app nahi chhodta.
- **Support kam:** common sawalon ke jawab lessons me hote hain, to resellers ko baar baar samjhana nahi padta.
- **Upsell:** course ke andar natural tareeke se premium features dikhao (jaise forecasting wale lesson me Growth plan ka suggestion).
- **Alag revenue:** basic courses free rakho aur advanced ya certification courses paid ya premium plan me do.
- **Reseller ke liye:** wahi courses resellers ke liye bhi training bante hain ("Mess ko kaise bechein").

## Aur kya jod sakte ho

- **Weekly tip card:** dashboard par roz ek chhoti seekh ("aaj ka tip").
- **Community:** owners ke liye ek group ya Q&A jahan woh ek doosre se seekh sakein.
- **Success stories:** kisi mess ka "pehle aur baad" ka chhota case study.
- **Personalised suggestion:** agar owner ka wastage zyada hai to app apne aap wastage wala course suggest kare.

## Shuru kaise karein (sasta tareeka)

1. Pehle sirf **2-3 course** banao, aur content tum khud ya kisi accha mess chalane wale owner ke saath mil kar record karo.
2. Videos YouTube (unlisted) par rakho aur app me embed karo, jisse hosting ka kharcha zero rahe.
3. Lesson ke end me quiz ya task rakho, aur progress database me save karo.
4. Owners ke feedback se dekho kaun se lessons sabse zyada dekhe jaate hain, aur wahi aur banao.

Agar chaho to main ek course ka poora structure aur pehle course ke 6 lessons ki script (Hindi me) likh doon, ya app me is Academy section ka ek working demo bana doon.
